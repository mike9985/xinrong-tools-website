About Xinrong

Two decades of manufacturing excellence in lithium power tools

Our Story

Xinrong Electric Tools (Weifang) Co., Ltd. was established in 2005 in Weifang, Shandong Province — one of China's key manufacturing hubs for power tools and machinery.

For over 20 years, we have specialized in the research, development, and manufacturing of professional-grade lithium battery power tools. Our product portfolio spans cordless tool sets, circular saws, rotary hammer drills, laser levels, and a wide range of accessories.

Today, our 8,000m² ISO 9001-certified factory houses advanced CNC machining centers, automated assembly lines, and a fully equipped testing laboratory. We serve distributors, importers, and brand owners in over 60 countries across Europe, the Middle East, Southeast Asia, Africa, and the Americas.

Our Journey

Key milestones in Xinrong's 20-year growth story.

2005

Company Founded

Xinrong Electric Tools established in Weifang, Shandong, with a focus on electric power tools manufacturing.

2010

European Market Entry

Obtained CE certification and began exporting to European markets. Established first long-term distribution partnerships in Germany and France.

2015

Factory Expansion

Moved to a new 8,000m² facility with upgraded CNC machining centers and automated assembly lines, tripling production capacity.

2020

ISO 9001 Certification

Achieved ISO 9001:2015 quality management certification. Launched in-house testing laboratory for comprehensive product validation.

2024

Global Reach

Now serving over 60 countries with 500+ product models. Expanded R&D team to strengthen ODM capabilities for custom tool development.
